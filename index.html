About us

A local bridge to global process technology.

Established in February 2012, PT Mulia Teknik Sejahtera Indo (MTSI) is managed by professionals with previous management experience in multinational companies. Our office is at Rukan Crown, Green Lake City Boulevard No. 12, Block K, Petir, Cipondoh, Tangerang, Banten 15147, Indonesia. Our mission is to provide commercial and technical support to end users across Indonesia.

We support EKATO reactor and large-scale agitators, mixing systems and engineering; Edwards vacuum pumps, ejectors and boosters; NEUMAN & ESSER compressor equipment, original parts and service; BHS thin-cake filtration, cake washing and drying; and LEWA pump enquiries. We serve oil and gas, petrochemicals, pharmaceuticals, fine chemicals, oleochemicals, biotechnology, refineries, palm oil, plastics and polymers, flue gas desulfurization, mining, and food industries.
